# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First build. Foundry VTT v14 only.

- Quest documents as module-defined `JournalEntryPage` subtypes, with tracks, milestones,
  item/currency/XP/custom rewards, quest giver and party assignment
- A GM vault holding unrevealed content, with one-click reveal and un-reveal
- Quest Log window: Job Board, Active, Completed, Failed, Abandoned, and a GM-only Drafts tab,
  with search, tag filters and right-click status and visibility menus
- Per-quest audience control: everyone, or named players
- Loot awarding with currency and XP splitting, per-character item assignment, and a preview
  of exactly what each person receives before anything is written
- System adapters for Nimble and D&D 5e, plus a generic adapter driven by configurable data paths
- JSON import and export of quests and chains, with a preview before anything is created and a
  player-safe export
- A GM-only story web: quests and free-form nodes on a pan/zoom canvas, with the quest chain
  derived from the quests themselves and editable by drawing links between cards
- Trusted players can propose quests of their own to track
